 ABOUT THE SYSTEM

EYENEX NEURAL

A modular AI command center prototype with two focused environments: Neural Core for commands and workflow planning, and Intelligence Lab for structured analysis.

This build includes a local mission planner, browser persistence, and optional camera-based head-pointer controls. Camera tracking is off until explicitly started in Settings.
