 ABOUT THE SYSTEM

EYENEX NEURAL

A modular AI command center prototype with two focused environments: Neural Core for commands and workflow planning, and Intelligence Lab for structured analysis.

This build includes a local mission planner and browser persistence. A live AI provider, external project access, autonomous execution, and camera-based tracking are not connected yet.
