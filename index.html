EYENEX NEURAL

A modular AI command center prototype with two focused environments: Neural Core for commands and workflow planning, and Intelligence Lab for structured analysis.

This repository currently contains a frontend concept. AI provider integration, project access, autonomous execution, and camera-based tracking are not connected yet.
